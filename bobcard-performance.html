The Brief

Challenges & Objective

Challenges

  • High CAC — Card Acquisition Cost over ₹5,000
  • Limited growth in online acquisition channels

Our Objective

  • Reduce CAC and increase brand & product awareness
  • Expand and optimise online acquisition channels
Our Approach

Multi-Channel Strategy

Our campaign strategy utilised a multi-channel approach, custom campaigns and various advertising platforms to maximise reach and efficiency.

  • Google Ads with both brand and non-brand keywords
  • Performance Max (PMax) with customised campaigns
  • Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) targeted by interest and audience
  • ORM to monitor and manage brand perception

Data-Led Audience Intelligence

First-Party Data

Integrated first-party data into the user journey for smooth audience traffic and accurate conversion tracking.

Social Listening

Monitored over 15,000+ mentions via Locobuzz to establish brand sentiment and created 10+ audience profiles.

Campaign Customisation

Campaigns customised per audience set and matched with product features and offerings for maximum relevance.

Distribution Channel Selection

Carefully selected channels to ensure the right message reached the right audience at the right time.

Campaign Execution

The Process

01
Product Understanding
Thoroughly understood each card product and its unique offerings before campaign creation.
02
Target Group Identification
Identified target groups that best matched each product's offerings based on demographics, interests, and job profiles.
03
Social Listening via Locobuzz
Monitored social channels, scanning over 15,000 engagement mediums to gauge brand sentiment and awareness.
04
Audience Segmentation
Created distinct segments matched with the most suitable credit cards based on demographics, interests, and social media patterns.
05
Customised Campaign Creation
Crafted campaigns tailored to each audience including topical ones to tap into audience interests and trends.
06
Distribution Channel Selection
Strategically selected channels to ensure ads remained relevant to the right audience at the right time.
07
Continuous Optimisation
Regularly analysed keywords, added negatives, refreshed creatives to prevent fatigue, and ran brand awareness campaigns.

Campaign Sustenance

Optimisation Strategy

Keyword Analysis

Regularly analysed performance to identify high-converting keywords and added negatives to filter irrelevant traffic.

Creative Refresh

Communication revamp based on creative fatigue reports. Brand campaigns ensured continued awareness.

Product-Specific Campaigns

Individual campaigns per card product — highlighting airport lounge access, reward points, and unique features.

Feature-Specific Targeting

Targeted ads based on key card features, delivering the right message to the right audience at the right time.

Google Ad Customisation

Dedicated campaigns for In-market and Custom segments based on audience profiles identified via social listening.

Continuous Learning Loop

Every campaign iteration fed back into the strategy — audience signals, creative performance, and channel data continuously refined the approach.
