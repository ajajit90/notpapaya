Increasing Credit Card Acquisition Through Performance Marketing

ClientBOBCARD
Duration10 Months
The Brief

Challenges & Objective

Challenges

  • High initial Cost Per Conversion — Card Acquisition Cost (CAC) over ₹5,000
  • Limited growth in online acquisition channels

Our Objective

  • Reduce CAC and increase brand & product awareness
  • Expand and optimise online acquisition channels
Our Approach

Multi-Channel Strategy

Our campaign strategy utilised a multi-channel approach, custom campaigns and various advertising platforms to maximise reach and efficiency.

  • Google Ads with both brand and non-brand keywords
  • Performance Max (PMax) with customised campaigns
  • Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram) targeted as per the interest and likeness of the audience
  • Online Reputation Management (ORM) to monitor and manage brand perception

Our Approach

Data-Led Audience Intelligence

First-Party Data Integration

We integrated first-party data into the user journey to ensure smooth audience traffic flow and accurate conversion tracking.

Social Listening

Through social listening, we monitored over 15,000+ mentions and engagements to establish brand sentiment and created 10+ audience profiles to understand which product fits which audience.

Campaign Customisation

Campaigns were customised as per the audience set and matched with product features and offerings for maximum relevance.

Distribution Channel Selection

Carefully selected distribution channels to ensure the right message reached the right audience at the right time.

Campaign Sustenance

Optimisation Strategy

Keyword Performance Analysis

Regularly analysed keyword performance to identify high-converting keywords and added negative keywords to filter out irrelevant traffic.

Creative Refresh

Creative and communication revamp based on creative fatigue reports. Brand campaigns ensured continued awareness and brand following.

Product-Specific Campaigns

Created individual campaigns tailored to each card product — highlighting airport lounge access, reward points, and unique features to attract the right audience.

Feature-Specific Targeting

Targeted ads based on key card features, delivering ads to the right audience at the right time — enhancing relevance and campaign effectiveness.

Google Ad Customisation

Launched dedicated campaigns for In-market and Custom segments. In-market segments targeted active searchers; Custom segments were tailored to unique profiles identified via social listening.

Continuous Learning Loop

Every campaign iteration fed back into the strategy — audience signals, creative performance, and channel data continuously refined the approach.
