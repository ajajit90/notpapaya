About

I started in advertising when ideas lived on paper and died on TV. I've written scripts that aired nationally, pitched campaigns that won awards, and built brands from a blank brief.

Over the last seven years, I moved into the digital world — not as a pivot, but as an expansion. Performance marketing, content strategy, SEO, social — I learned them the way I learned copywriting: by doing the work.

Today I lead MARCOM at Intelegencia Digital — managing strategy, teams, and client accounts across India, the US, and the Philippines. I'm the person who can talk to a creative director and a media buyer in the same meeting and make sense to both.
