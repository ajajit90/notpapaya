The Brief

Client & Challenge

About the Client

BOBCard is a leading credit card brand under Bank of Baroda, focused on customer acquisition, feature-led positioning, and rapid user response within a highly competitive banking and fintech ecosystem.

Key Challenges

  • Critically low Play Store ratings eroding user trust
  • Minimal organic visibility on App Store & Play Store
  • Ineffective keyword strategy limiting discoverability
  • 16.3% complaints with zero brand response — hurting conversions
The Solution

Four-Pillar Strategy

01
Canned Response System
Built structured reply templates for appreciation, complaints, and feedback — ensuring consistent, professional, on-brand communication at scale across 1,578 tickets.
02
Proactive Review Engagement
Delivered timely, personalised responses to all user reviews, achieving an 89.6% response rate. Addressed both praise and pain points within a defined SLA.
03
Store Visual Optimisation
Optimised app preview images across Play Store & App Store to improve branding clarity and communicate key features to prospective users.
04
Keyword Benchmarking & ASO
Benchmarked brand and non-brand keywords against competitors; optimised store listings for improved organic search rankings and discoverability.
